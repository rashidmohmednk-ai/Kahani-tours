About Kahani

A small office in Thamarassery, a world of stories.

Since 2021, Kahani Travel & Tours has helped families, friends, pilgrims and professionals from Kozhikode and beyond travel well.

Kahani means story, and we chose the name on purpose. We think every trip should be one worth telling: the first sight of Dal Lake at dawn, a houseboat drifting through the Alleppey backwaters, the stillness of the Haram at Fajr, a child's face when the Burj Khalifa lights up. We look after the small, important details so you can enjoy the parts worth remembering.

We started with tickets and visas and grew into a full-service travel house. We plan international holidays and Indian journeys, look after Kerala getaways, guide pilgrims through Umrah, and handle the paperwork that comes before any trip abroad: passports, visa stamping and certificate attestation, through the right offices and done properly.

“Come in for a chai and a chat, bring your documents, or simply tell us where you've always wanted to go. We'll take care of the rest.”

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions.

Can't find what you're looking for? Call us on +91 99465 00546 or send an enquiry.

Where is Kahani Travel & Tours located?

Our office is at Dr KKMK Complex, near Bishop's House, Thamarassery, Kozhikode (Calicut), Kerala. You are welcome to visit us, or call +91 99465 00546 / +91 96332 00550.

Do you arrange tour packages from Kozhikode and Calicut?

Yes. We plan international holidays to Dubai, Thailand, Malaysia, Sri Lanka, Vietnam, Georgia, Azerbaijan and Kazakhstan, Indian trips to Kashmir, Kullu Manali, Andaman, Lakshadweep, Delhi, Goa, Rajasthan and Ooty, and Kerala packages to Munnar, Alleppey, Wayanad, Thekkady, Vagamon, Fort Kochi and Varkala. Flights can start from Calicut (CCJ), Kochi or any city you prefer.

Do you provide certificate attestation for the UAE, Saudi, Qatar, Oman and Kuwait?

Yes. We handle HRD, Home Department, Notary, MEA and embassy attestation for academic, commercial, birth, marriage, death and divorce certificates and power of attorney.

Can you help with visas and passports?

Yes. We assist with employment visa stamping and tourist, visit and business visas, as well as passport applications, renewals, police clearance certificates (PCC) and medical appointments.

Do you offer Umrah packages?

Yes. We offer group, family and customised Umrah packages including visa, flights, hotels near the Haram, and Ziyarat in Makkah and Madinah.

Are you an authorised railway ticketing agent?

Yes. We are an Indian Railways authorised ticketing agent, and we also book KSRTC and private bus tickets.
